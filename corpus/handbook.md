Coursework 1 is due on 14 November at 12:00 and is worth 40% of the module mark.
Submission is through the module page as a single PDF.

Coursework 2 is due on 16 January at 12:00 and is worth 60% of the module mark.
It is submitted in the same way.

Feedback on coursework 1 is returned within fifteen working days of the deadline.
